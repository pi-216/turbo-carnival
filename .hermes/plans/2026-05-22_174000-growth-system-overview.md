## Implementation Order

1. **Technical QA** (first) — blocks blurry images. Immediate value.
2. **Auto-Selection** — ranks by quality, picks winners. Enables autonomous posting.
3. **Feedback Loop** — learns from engagement. Needs ~7 days data. Run weekly.
4. **Creative Exploration** — monthly test pillars. Needs Feedback Loop data to evaluate.

Phases 1+2 ship together. Phase 3 activates after 1 week of posts. Phase 4 starts after Phase 3 has 1 month of data.
